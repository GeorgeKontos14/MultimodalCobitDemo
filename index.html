Multimodal Co-Generation with Continuous Bitstream Diffusion

Georgios Batzolis Georgios Kontos Bruno Trentini Pranam Chatterjee Micha Livne Mark Girolami Luca Ambrogioni
Paper ↗ Github ↗

Abstract

Continuous bitstream diffusion (CoBit), first introduced for language modeling, is applied here as a unified framework for joint and conditional multimodal generation. Separate image–text, speech–text, and protein sequence–structure models share the same core architecture, binary score-matching objective, and sampling procedure across modalities. Within each setting, changing which portions of the bitstream are observed lets one checkpoint support multiple generation directions. Experiments span raw-pixel MNIST-SUM, speech synthesis and recognition, and protein folding and design. Jointly generated speech and text reach 3.5% cross-modal word error rate, while jointly generated proteins achieve a mean self-consistency TM-score of 0.911—all without a domain-specific objective or sampler.
